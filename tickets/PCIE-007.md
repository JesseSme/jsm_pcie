## Deliverables and behavior

- [ ] Integrate a solderable discrete-transistor clocked serializer only after PCIE-045 building-block evaluation, following docs/discrete-transistor-serializer-plan.md; record any rejected route without silently substituting an IC.
- [ ] Map arbitrary 10-bit symbols to phase/data schedules with explicit setup/hold, duty cycle, phase noise and 2.5 GT/s throughput budgets.
- [ ] Include differential drive, amplitude control, de-emphasis, idle behavior and startup transients; do not substitute an RF carrier for PCIe baseband NRZ.
- [ ] Reject candidates that only generate a high-frequency periodic clock or depend on undocumented FPGA overclocking.
- [ ] Constrain simulated FPGA pins to GW2AR-18 supported gearbox rates, finite edge times and selected-bank electrical limits; any 2.5 GT/s output must be generated by explicitly modeled external circuitry.
- [ ] Design the approved clocked external serialization path; start with four FPGA streams at 625 Mb/s into a 4:1 serializer and model the complete output stage.
- [ ] Generate external capture/select timing through a justified clocking circuit rather than assuming high-speed mux select can be driven directly by ordinary FPGA logic.
- [ ] Use staggered branch retiming and qualified clock phase distribution; prevent first-stage update races through the final 2:1 mux, including group-boundary changes.
- [ ] Derive the serializer bit clock and select phases from the PCIE-003 reference-clock architecture; a laboratory instrument clock is permitted only for coupon experiments.
- [ ] Do not begin full-serializer PCB work until PCIE-046 records a receive-path recommendation; a working transmitter without a feasible receiver does not produce a link.
